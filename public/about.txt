The site logo, OG card and favicon are hand-authored SVGs in this repo:

- public/images/site-logo.svg       wordmark, dark theme
- public/images/site-logo-light.svg wordmark, light theme
- public/images/og-card.svg         social card; og-card.png is rendered from it
- public/favicon.svg                app icon; the .ico and .png sizes are rendered from it

The PNG sizes were rasterised from those SVGs with headless Chrome at
16, 32, 180, 192 and 512 pixels. Re-render them if the SVGs change.
